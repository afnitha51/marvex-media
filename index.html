QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about working with Marvex Media. Can't find your answer? Contact us directly.

Getting Started

Starting is simple. Click "Get Started" or head to our Contact page and fill in the project brief form. One of our consultants will reach out within 24 hours to schedule a free discovery call. No pressure, no commitment at this stage — just a conversation.

Yes — we offer a complimentary 45-minute discovery call for every new project inquiry. During this session we scope your requirements, discuss your goals, and provide an honest assessment of the best approach. No sales pitch, just real advice.

It helps to have a rough idea of: (1) what problem you're trying to solve, (2) your target audience, (3) your budget range, and (4) your desired timeline. Even rough answers are fine — we'll help you refine everything during the call.

Pricing & Timelines

Pricing varies by scope. A professional marketing website typically starts at AED 8,000–18,000. A custom web application or mobile app usually starts at AED 25,000+. We provide detailed, transparent quotes after our discovery call — no vague estimates.

A standard marketing website takes 3–6 weeks. A complex web application or mobile app typically takes 8–20 weeks depending on features. We always provide a detailed project timeline in your proposal, with milestones and delivery dates agreed upfront.

Yes. We typically structure payments in milestones: 30% upfront, 40% at midpoint, and 30% on delivery. For larger projects, we can arrange custom payment schedules. We accept bank transfer (UAE, Qatar, India), Wise, and major cards.

Process & Collaboration

Every client gets a dedicated project manager and access to a shared Notion workspace where you can see real-time progress, timelines, and deliverables. We hold weekly check-in calls and provide written updates every Friday. You'll never be left wondering what's happening.

Absolutely. Our design phase happens before any development work. You'll receive Figma mockups for every key screen and we iterate until you're 100% satisfied. Development only starts once designs are signed off.

All projects include a 30-day post-launch support window at no extra cost. After that, we offer flexible maintenance retainers starting at AED 1,500/month, covering updates, bug fixes, security patches, and performance monitoring.

Technical & Hosting

You own all code and assets — fully. We can deploy to your preferred hosting provider (Vercel, AWS, DigitalOcean, etc.) or manage hosting on your behalf. We recommend solutions that give you the best performance for your region and traffic levels.

Always. Mobile-first, responsive design is standard on every project. We target 90+ Google PageSpeed scores across mobile and desktop, and conduct cross-browser testing before launch. Speed is a priority — it directly impacts your SEO and conversion rates.

We use battle-tested open-source stacks (React, Next.js, Flutter, Node.js, Python) wherever possible so you're never locked into our services. Your codebase is clean, documented, and transferable — you can take it to any developer in the future.
